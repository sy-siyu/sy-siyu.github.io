  1. ICML2026

    Reward-Preserving Counterfactual State Editing for Offline Reinforcement Learning

    Siyu Wang, Xiaocong Chen, Mingming Gong, Yong Li, Quan Z. Sheng, Lina Yao

    Offline policies can rely on misleading correlations. We edit recorded states while preserving predicted rewards, helping policies focus on causally relevant information.

    Read paper ↗
  2. ICML2026

    Continuity-Regularized Flow Matching for Offline Reinforcement Learning

    Xiaocong Chen, Siyu Wang, Lina Yao

    Generative policies can follow unstable paths from noise to actions. We regularise these paths to make offline policy learning smoother and more stable.

    Read paper ↗
  3. WWW2025

    Policy-Guided Causal State Representation for Offline Reinforcement Learning Recommendation

    Siyu Wang, Xiaocong Chen, Lina Yao

    Recommendation logs contain irrelevant and noisy features. We learn compact state representations that retain information causally relevant to reward.

About me

I’m a Postdoctoral Fellow at UNSW Sydney, where I also completed my PhD in Computer Science and Engineering. Before joining UNSW in my current role, I worked at Macquarie University.

I enjoy the collaborative side of research: turning an initial question into a clear problem, testing ideas carefully, and understanding what the results tell us. When mentoring research students, I aim to help them develop independent thinking, thoughtful experiments and clear scientific communication.
