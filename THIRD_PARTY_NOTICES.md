## Personal illustrations

The cartoon portrait was generated specifically from the site owner's supplied photo, then edited at her request with a different hairstyle and outfit, a more natural head and neck posture, and a simplified hand-drawn cartoon style. The user's sample illustrations informed the visual simplification only; their people, props and backgrounds are not used on the website. It is not an open-source stock character. The original photo is unchanged.

The Sydney Harbour and Contact envelope illustrations are original generated paintings, guided by the user's minimal, painterly style reference. The Sydney composition is based on the view from Mrs Macquarie's Point, with the Opera House in the left foreground and the Harbour Bridge behind and to the right. A [Botanic Gardens of Sydney reference photo](https://www.botanicgardens.org.au/best-photo-locations-botanic-gardens-sydney) was consulted for landmark placement; that photograph is not distributed with this website. The illustration is an abstract interpretation, not an architectural or geographic document.

Earlier cartoon, harbour, garden and notebook illustrations remain in the source assets for reference but are not included in the current preview output.

The current illustrations are delivered as lossless WebP files. Their decoded RGBA pixels and dimensions match the original PNGs, which remain in the source assets.

The latest cartoon revision preserves the character's raised hand, blue shirt and loose bun, with warmer pencil edges and softer painted texture to match the harbour and envelope. The envelope was edited into an empty painted shell; complementary CSS-clipped layers surround a separate paper slip for its one-time greeting animation. Both asset edits used built-in image generation. Previous versions remain in the source assets. Motion is disabled when the visitor prefers reduced motion.

The homepage's animated harbour water lines are original inline SVG accents. Ambient portrait and water movement runs only while the artwork is visible and the browser tab is active. The portrait is a keyboard-accessible motion toggle; its preference is stored locally in the visitor's browser when available. The contact paper greeting replays on section re-entry. No third-party animation service, tracking script or new image generation is used for these effects.
